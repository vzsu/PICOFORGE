### 11:20 · engine: min-wall probe by distance transform (ENG-MINWALL-01..03, ENG-MINWALL-perf)
- Why: the probe was 74–91 % of sandbox time (8 meshed erosions per build).
- Change: `Stats.MinWallProbe(v, voxelMm)` reads the signed field slice by slice (`GetVoxelSlice`, SignedDistance), runs one separable exact EDT (Felzenszwalb–Huttenlocher), and takes the 15 % erosion rule as a quantile of the distance histogram. The old method is kept as `MinWallProbeByErosion`, the test reference. `InsideVolumeMm3` uses partial occupancy.
- LESSON: PicoGK slice SignedDistance is in **mm**, clamped to ±3 voxels. A box on a voxel-aligned grid has centres exactly on the surface (sd = 0). Counting those as inside gave (40.25)²×2.25 = 3645 mm³ instead of 3200 (first red). Fixes: inside = sd < 0 strictly; volume = Σ clamp(½ − sd/vox, 0, 1); surface offset c = mean sd of boundary outside centres, instead of a fixed ½.
- Test setup fix: the draft test called a nonexistent `Kit.voxGyroidInfill`. Replaced it with `voxFromImplicit(impGyroid(10, 1.2), 40³ box)`, the same as the bench design. No assertion changed.
